5–6 hours. No coding background. My personal portfolio is live.

I wanted a space that brought together my work, creativity, and the experiences that have shaped me.

The hardest parts were choosing a design that felt like me and figuring out which software to use. Every choice raised another question: what should people see first, which projects should I highlight, and which photographs actually add to the story?

I used AI to help with the code and technical setup. I provided my story, projects, photographs, and creative direction, then worked through the choices and revisions along the way.

Even preparing images, uploading files, and understanding how to publish and share the website became part of the learning process.

As a marketing student, this became a practical exercise in personal branding. The words, visuals, and order of information all influence how someone understands you.

The result is “The Human Variable”: a portfolio connecting my work in content, brand partnerships, and consumer research with the people and experiences behind it.
